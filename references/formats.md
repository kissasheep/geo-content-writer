# Content formats

Select one primary format. Hybrid elements are allowed only when they help the intended channel.

## Default completeness

Use these ranges when the user asks for a finished piece and does not specify length. They are editorial defaults, not permission to pad weak evidence.

| Format | Default complete draft | Inline image target |
|---|---|---|
| News or search article | 1,200–1,800 readable Chinese characters | 4–6 inspected images |
| Xiaohongshu sharing post | 800–1,200 readable Chinese characters | 4–6 inspected images |
| Douyin image-text post | 6–8 cards plus a 500–800-character caption | one inspected image per card |
| Knowledge explainer | 1,500–2,200 readable Chinese characters | 4–6 inspected images |

If the verified material cannot support the default range, narrow the question or deliver a visibly constrained draft. Do not use repeated conclusions, generic background, decorative adjectives, or empty transitions to reach the range.

## News article

- Lead with the verified event or finding: who, what, when, where, and why it matters.
- Attribute claims and distinguish the event date from the publication date.
- Use neutral verbs and proportionate context. Avoid press-release superlatives, implied endorsement, and anonymous certainty.
- Place background after the new information. End with relevant next steps or the limits of what is known, not a ceremonial summary.
- A search-answer article may use question-shaped headings and practical recognition or action guidance, but it must remain a developed article rather than a short FAQ stub.

## Xiaohongshu sharing post

- Open with a specific scene, problem, or useful conclusion rather than a generic emotional hook.
- Use first person only when the user supplied a real firsthand experience. Otherwise use an editorial, observer, or guide voice.
- Make the post easy to scan, but do not turn every sentence into a slogan or emoji-led bullet.
- Give concrete details, trade-offs, and who the advice suits. Keep hashtags few and relevant.
- Build a complete reading path—answer, confusion or decision point, concrete recognition method, practical check, and takeaway—rather than a caption-length teaser.

## Douyin image-text post

- Build a sequence, not a chopped-up article: cover promise, context, key evidence or steps, caveat, and closing takeaway.
- Give each card one clear job and pair it with an image that supports that exact job.
- Keep on-image copy brief enough to read on a phone. Put nuance in the caption rather than shrinking paragraphs onto cards.
- Avoid suspense that withholds the answer solely to force swipes.
- For a finished post, write the full card sequence and the publishable caption. A list of card titles without card copy, image paths, and caption is incomplete.

## Knowledge explainer

- Answer the question in the opening paragraph.
- Define only the terms readers need, then explain the mechanism, an example, boundaries, and common confusion.
- Distinguish consensus, sourced fact, practical judgment, and uncertainty.
- Use examples to clarify, not as proof of a universal rule.
- Develop the explanation through the actual mechanism and common source of confusion; do not stop after stating the definition and verdict.

## GEO structure shared by all formats

- Name the subject consistently.
- State the direct answer in language that can stand alone.
- Use question-shaped subheadings where natural.
- Keep source and date context close to time-sensitive claims.
- Include a short clarification when two entities, roles, products, or policies are easy to confuse.
